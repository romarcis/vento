## Direction contract
THESIS: Vento is an appliance, not a dashboard: the fan curve is the whole screen, and the live operating point rides it like a dial pointer. Refuses the equal-weight widget grid and RGB-gamer chrome.
OWN-WORLD: Dark warm graphite ground (not black), one signal orange for user-authored things (draft curve, selection), pale green/amber/red only as state. Archivo variable (width axis: condensed caps for legends, normal for readouts), tabular numerals. Braun-catalog discipline: matte, hairline scale ticks, tiny radii, flat.
STORY: Open window, see 5 fans and 6 sensors live, pick a fan, drag its curve, see the draft against the applied curve, apply or revert, close to tray.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (wordmark, profile switch, apply/revert). Sensor strip of 6 readouts. Left rail of 5 fans (RPM large). Centre ~70%: SVG curve editor with live operating point, 60s range band, ghost of applied curve; point inputs row beneath.
FORM: Braun/Olivetti industrial-design catalog (own grounded candidate 7, seed a712fcfa), lending type, palette, density and the dial-pointer signature move only; controls stay standard.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
